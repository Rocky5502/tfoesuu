\caption{The P18 replay phase transition.  Synchronized replay is linearly
sensitive to a differential effect only while the fairness-permitted background

creates ordinary interior Bernoulli noise and restores quadratic local testing
complexity.  The figure summarizes the proved one-edge theorem; it contains no
empirical estimates.}
